The Reef

Close your eyes. You are five years old, and the ocean has just shown you something you will never forget.

" ...you see.
This is what is happening to the reef. "
Over the last thirty years, warming and acidifying oceans have bleached and killed vast stretches of coral reef around the world. What took thousands of years to grow can disappear in a single hot summer.
